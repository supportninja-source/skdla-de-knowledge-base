ABS Case Entry Policy & Procedure

Version 3.5 • Start-to-finish procedure for entering an eligible digital case into ABS, from pre-entry verification through Rx upload, QC, and tracker completion.

← Back to Knowledge Base

Current confirmed Ninja scope: standard 1-unit Zirconia Crown cases. Implant/Screw Retained cases route to Orange County. If a required rule is unclear or pending, stop and escalate rather than guessing.
1

Receive the case through the current assignment workflow

Obtain/claim the case through ClearPath using the current team process, then open the correct source PDF/work order and confirm you are working the correct patient/case. Detailed ClearPath grab-case steps will be added after the official procedure is provided.

2

Confirm current Ninja scope

Confirm the case is within the current authorized Data Entry scope: standard 1-unit Zirconia Crown. If implant, Screw Retained, or otherwise outside scope, route to OC/support.

3

Check required Rx information

Verify doctor, tooth number, shade, restoration request, requested due date, and special instructions. If required information is missing or unclear, do not guess.

4

Open the required ABS windows

Open New Order, Case Inquiry, and Case Photos.

5

Perform the duplicate/history check

In Case Inquiry, search the patient and doctor/practice. Review previous cases, especially same-tooth/restoration history. Do not create a new case when duplicate/remake status is unclear.

6

Start the new order

After the duplicate check passes, click New in New Order.

7

Verify doctor and practice

Match the doctor/practice against the Rx, 3Shape, address/office information, and Notes/Comments. For Aspen, use the collaborator/office number and select the matching Aspen/OCASP office, not Beacon.

8

Enter the patient name

Use the approved naming rules: a fully spelled middle name stays with First Name; two last names stay together in Last Name.

9

Review the Rx again

Confirm treatment/restoration, tooth, shade, due date, special instructions, implant/Screw Retained indicators, and model/product clues.

10

Enter requested due date and shade

Enter the doctor-requested due date and shade exactly from the Rx. Do not replace them with a default suggestion.

11

Select product/service

Go to Services > Lab, select the appropriate Digital Models service, and choose the product/material allowed by the current scope.

12

Select the tooth

Choose the exact tooth number and click Save Selection.

13

Select the model

Check Doctor Preferences and account rules first. For Incisive cases, do not use Mini models; use Quadrant or Full Arch according to the Incisive visual model-selection guide. Incisive anterior/incisor uses Full Arch; anterior + posterior uses Full Arch; three posterior teeth on opposing sides use Full Arch; and two posterior teeth on the same arch/next to each other use Quadrant. For non-Incisive standard eligible posterior cases, a 3D Printed Mini Model may still apply when no exception exists. OCIN/account-specific rules and Doctor Preferences must also be followed.

14

Set the workflow to Digital

Select Digital, not Analog. If Digital is missing, reopen/restart ABS and escalate if it remains unavailable.

15

Enter case notes

Enter the current date, initials, Rx requested due date when required, and approved clarifications/observations.

16

Generate the schedule

Open Digital Workflow/View Schedule and click Generate Schedule. Review the generated dates; do not manually force the schedule.

17

Handle schedule exceptions

For confirmed OCIN guidance: a one-day-short schedule may proceed. If the delivery date is five days or less away and ABS says the schedule will be short by two days, click the square "No due date" button under Due Date, allow ABS to create the preferred delivery due date, click Generate for the new schedule, and add the note: "Yareli to call for new delivery date of [ABS-set delivery date]." If the delivery date is more than five days away, follow the separate more-than-five-days due-date exception procedure. For Aspen cases, exactly one day short may proceed; if the schedule is short by two days or more, stop normal processing and escalate.

18

Save and obtain the ABS case number

Click Continue, then Save. Confirm the unique ABS case number appears. If ABS crashes first, reopen and check Case Inquiry before re-entering.

19

Upload the Rx

Open Case Photos, locate the correct case, upload the actual Rx form PDF, and verify the correct document is visible. Workticket alone is not sufficient.

20

Perform final QC

Recheck doctor/practice, patient, duplicate check, scope/routing, product/material, tooth, model, shade, requested due date, notes, Digital schedule, case number, and Rx upload.

21

Complete or reroute the case

If QC passes, complete the case according to the current approved ClearPath/team workflow. If the case requires escalation/OC or documentation is incomplete, use the approved route/disposition instead.

Official guidance overrides older Training Guidance. Pending Confirmation items are not official instructions. Customer Service procedures are outside this document.
